# Simulation Logs (Zenodo)

The raw live-simulation logs referenced by the paper are archived on Zenodo as a
citable dataset. The two large logs are hosted on Zenodo only. The seven smaller
LLM-synthesis logs are also mirrored in this repository.

**Zenodo DOI (all versions):** [10.5281/zenodo.21151213](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21151213)

The concept DOI above always resolves to the latest version.

**Contents:**

| File | Size | Births | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sim_log_mendelian_haploid.json` | ~74 MB | 1,278 | Mendelian haploid run. Includes the action log, so it serves both the inheritance and marker-decay analyses. |
| `sim_log_diploid_codominant.json` | ~74 MB | 1,254 | Diploid co-dominant run. Includes the action log, so it serves both the inheritance and marker-decay analyses. |
| `sim_log_llm_synthesis_free_action.json` | 6 MB | 39 | LLM synthesis, free action |
| `sim_log_llm_synthesis_predator_bloom_locked.json` | 5 MB | 47 | LLM synthesis, predator pressure |
| `sim_log_llm_synthesis_abundance_locked.json` | 4 MB | 43 | LLM synthesis, abundance scenario |
| `sim_log_llm_synthesis_free_epoch.json` | 2 MB | 45 | LLM synthesis, free epoch, baseline |
| `sim_log_llm_synthesis_free_epoch_run1.json` | 2 MB | 52 | LLM synthesis, free epoch, seed 1 |
| `sim_log_llm_synthesis_free_epoch_run2.json` | 2 MB | 52 | LLM synthesis, free epoch, seed 2 |
| `sim_log_llm_synthesis_free_regulated_pop.json` | 2 MB | 35 | LLM synthesis, population-regulated |

The two large logs are the monolithic form of the chunked
`sim_log_mendelian_haploid_0N.json` and `sim_log_diploid_codominant_0N.json`
files committed in this repository (the analysis scripts load the committed
chunks). The reproduction does not require the Zenodo logs.

**Reproducing the logs:** they are generated by the live evolutionary-ecosystem
simulation in the BEAR library. The paper evaluations run from the pre-recorded
logs committed here and do not require re-running the simulation.
